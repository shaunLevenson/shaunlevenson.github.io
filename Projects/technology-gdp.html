Historical performance through 2024, forecasts for 2025-2029

Research question and scope

This project examines how capital, labor, and sector-specific dynamics contribute to US technology-sector output. The sector is defined as an identity of NAICS 334: Computer and Electronic Products Manufacturing plus NAICS 51: Information.

The analysis combines historical review, a theoretical production function, and competing forecasting approaches to compare how different assumptions shape the technology sector's outlook.

Key finding

The structural model reinforced the central role of capital and labor as driving forces in the technology sector's production function. Inertial models are fast and often provide reasonable forecasts, but they do not capture the nuanced dynamics represented by the structural model.
