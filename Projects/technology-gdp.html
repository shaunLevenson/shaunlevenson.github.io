Project Type: Econometric Research

US Technology Sector GDP Forecast

A production function approach leveraging a multi-equation model to forecast the GDP attributed to the US technology sector.

Date: Aug 2024 - Aug 2025 Tools: Excel, EViews, R, Powerpoint, Word Role: Independent Researcher Mentor: Dan Hamilton

Overview

With the rise of AI and its overarching impact on the economy, the technology sector's contribution to society has been paramount. With this project, I aim to provide a comprehensive analysis of the historical performance, the theoretical production function, and the future outlook of the United States technology sector. The technology sector is defined as an identity of NAICS 334: Computer and Electronic Products Manufacturing plus NAICS 51: Information.

Process

  • Findings

    The structural model reinforced the central role of capital and labor as driving forces in the technology sector's production function. Inertial modeling techniques are fast and often provide reasonable forecasts, but fail to capture the nuanced dynamics that structural models hold inherent in their design.
